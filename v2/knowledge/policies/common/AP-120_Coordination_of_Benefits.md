# AP-120 Coordination of Benefits (COB) and Medicare Secondary Payer
Applies to: all AnyHealth plans · Synthetic demo policy

- When item 11d is YES or items 9/9a/9d are completed, another payer may be primary. AnyHealth pays as secondary
  only after receiving the primary payer's EOB or remittance; without it the claim is pended (CARC 22 this care
  may be covered by another payer; RARC MA04).
- Birthday rule for dependent children covered by both parents: the plan of the parent whose birthday falls earlier
  in the calendar year is primary.
- Medicare: if item 1 marks MEDICARE, confirm the member's Medicare entitlement. For members under 65 with
  employer group coverage, AnyHealth is generally primary; the provider must correct item 1 (pend, CARC 16 /
  RARC MA04).
- The amount paid by another payer is reported in item 29 and reduces AnyHealth's liability.
