# MP-209 Evaluation and Management (E/M) Services and Modifier 25
Applies to: all AnyHealth plans · Synthetic demo policy · Codes: 99202-99215

- Level of service is supported by medical decision making or total time on the date of service. 99214 requires
  moderate MDM (e.g. one chronic illness with exacerbation, or two stable chronic illnesses, with prescription drug
  management) or 30-39 minutes.
- Modifier **25** is required when an E/M is billed on the same date as a procedure or test by the same provider
  and must be supported by a significant, separately identifiable problem documented in the note.
- Telehealth E/M requires modifier **95** and POS 02 or 10 (see AP-150). Modifier 95 with an office POS (11) is
  an inconsistency and the line is pended for correction (CARC 16, RARC M77).
- Only one E/M per member, per provider specialty, per date is payable.
