# MP-206 Cardiac Diagnostic Testing
Applies to: all AnyHealth plans · Synthetic demo policy · Codes: 93000-93010 (ECG), 93015-93018 (stress test),
93306-93308 (transthoracic echocardiography)

## ECG (93000)
Covered for evaluation of chest pain (R07.x), palpitations, syncope, known coronary artery disease, angina (I20.x),
arrhythmia, or pre-operative evaluation.

## Exercise or pharmacologic stress test (93015-93018)
Covered for suspected coronary artery disease with intermediate pre-test probability: chest pain (R07.89, R07.9),
angina (I20.x) or an abnormal resting ECG. Not covered for routine screening of asymptomatic members.
93017 (tracing only) is billed by the facility performing the test; 93016/93018 by the supervising/interpreting
physician. Prior authorization is required under AnyHealth Standard.

## Transthoracic echocardiography (93306)
Covered for suspected structural heart disease, heart failure, murmur, or chest pain with abnormal ECG or abnormal
stress test. Repeat studies within 12 months require documentation of new or worsening symptoms. Prior
authorization is required under AnyHealth Standard.
