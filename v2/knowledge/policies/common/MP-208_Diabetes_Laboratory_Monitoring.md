# MP-208 Diabetes Laboratory Monitoring
Applies to: all AnyHealth plans · Synthetic demo policy · Codes: 82043, 82570, 83036, 80053

- Urine albumin (82043) and urine creatinine (82570) for kidney disease screening are covered for members with
  diabetes mellitus (E10.x, E11.x, E13.x), including E11.29 type 2 diabetes with other diabetic kidney complication.
- Frequency: see each plan's Schedule of Benefits. Repeat tests on the same date must carry modifier **91**
  (repeat clinical diagnostic laboratory test) with a documented clinical reason; otherwise the repeat line is a
  duplicate (CARC 18).
- Units for 82043 represent separate specimens; more than 1 unit per date requires documentation.
- Laboratory services billed by a physician office require that the test was performed in the office (CLIA
  certificate) or item 20 "Outside lab" marked YES with the purchased service charge.
