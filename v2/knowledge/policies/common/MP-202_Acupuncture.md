# MP-202 Acupuncture
Applies to: plans where acupuncture is a covered benefit (AnyHealth Premium). Excluded under AnyHealth Plus and
AnyHealth Standard (see each plan's Schedule of Benefits). Synthetic demo policy · Codes: 97810-97814

## Covered indications (Premium)
- Chronic low back pain (> 12 weeks, M54.5x, M54.4x) and chronic neck pain (M54.2).
- Migraine prophylaxis (G43.x) after failure of one preventive medication.
- Chemotherapy-induced or pregnancy-related nausea and vomiting (R11.x, O21.x).

## Limits and documentation
- Up to 12 visits per plan year; prior authorization after visit 12.
- 97810/97813 are initial 15-minute codes and may be billed once per day; add-on codes 97811/97814 per additional
  15 minutes of personal one-on-one contact.
- Treatment must be performed by a licensed acupuncturist or a physician with acupuncture certification.
- Notes must document needle locations, total face-to-face time and response.

## Not covered
Acupuncture for indications not listed, for maintenance, or under plans where acupuncture is excluded (CARC 96,
RARC N130).
