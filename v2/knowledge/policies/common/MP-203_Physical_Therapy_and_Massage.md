# MP-203 Physical Therapy, Therapeutic Exercise and Massage
Applies to: all AnyHealth plans · Synthetic demo policy · Codes: 97110, 97112, 97124, 97140, 97161-97163

## Plan of care
Therapeutic services require a plan of care signed by the treating physician or licensed therapist with diagnosis,
long-term goals, type, amount, frequency and duration. Outpatient therapy lines must carry modifier **GP**
(physical therapy), **GO** (occupational) or **GN** (speech).

## Timed codes
97110, 97112, 97124 and 97140 are 15-minute units. Units follow the 8-minute rule (8-22 minutes = 1 unit,
23-37 = 2, 38-52 = 3, 53-67 = 4). Units billed must match documented minutes. More than 4 units of one timed code
on a date requires documentation of medical necessity.

## Massage therapy (97124)
Covered only when it is a component of an active PT or chiropractic plan of care for a documented musculoskeletal
condition and performed by a licensed therapist. Stand-alone massage, relaxation or wellness massage is not covered
(CARC 96). Massage and manual therapy (97140) on the same region on the same date are not separately payable.

## Visit limits
Visit limits per plan year are listed in each plan's Schedule of Benefits.
