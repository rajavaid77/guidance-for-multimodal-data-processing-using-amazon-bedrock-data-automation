# MP-207 Pulmonary Function Testing and Airway Clearance
Applies to: all AnyHealth plans · Synthetic demo policy · Codes: 94010-94060, 94668, 94669

## Pulmonary function testing
- 94060 (spirometry before and after bronchodilator) is covered for diagnosis or monitoring of asthma (J45.x),
  COPD (J44.x), unexplained dyspnea (R06.0x) or chronic cough (R05.x).
- Frequency limits are listed in each plan's Schedule of Benefits. Repeat testing on the same date requires
  modifier 76 (repeat by same physician) and a documented clinical reason.
- When the office performs only the technical component it must append TC; a separate interpretation (26) by
  another provider is billed by that provider.

## Mechanical chest wall oscillation (94669) and airway clearance (94668)
- Covered for COPD (J44.x) with documented daily sputum production, bronchiectasis (J47.x) or cystic fibrosis
  (E84.x). Not covered for asthma alone (J45.x) without one of these conditions.
- Must be ordered by a pulmonologist or the treating physician; units are per session.
