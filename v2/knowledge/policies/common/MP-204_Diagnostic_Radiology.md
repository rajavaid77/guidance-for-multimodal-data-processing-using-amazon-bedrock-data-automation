# MP-204 Diagnostic Radiology and Imaging Guidance
Applies to: all AnyHealth plans · Synthetic demo policy · Codes: 70010-76499 (radiography), 77002 (fluoroscopic guidance)

## Medical necessity
A radiographic study is covered when the ordering provider documents a clinical question and **the anatomic region
imaged matches the linked diagnosis** (24E pointer). Examples:
- Lumbar spine X-ray (72100, 72110) - low back pain (M54.5x), lumbar radiculopathy (M54.16), trauma.
- Hip/pelvis X-ray (73501-73523) - hip pain (M25.55x), hip osteoarthritis (M16.x), hip trauma.
- A hip X-ray (e.g. 73521) linked only to a non-specific back diagnosis such as M54.9 dorsalgia does **not** meet
  medical necessity unless the note documents hip symptoms (CARC 11 diagnosis inconsistent with procedure).

## Imaging guidance
77002 fluoroscopic guidance is separately payable only with a covered needle procedure (e.g. 20610 without
built-in guidance) on the same date. It is not payable with 20611 (includes ultrasound guidance) or alone.

## Professional/technical components
Modifier 26 (professional) or TC (technical) is required when only one component is performed. A global
service and a TC or 26 line for the same study and date is a duplicate (CARC 18).

## Advanced imaging
CT, MRI and PET require prior authorization under all plans.
