# MP-205 Joint Injection and Aspiration (Arthrocentesis)
Applies to: all AnyHealth plans · Synthetic demo policy · Codes: 20600-20611

- Covered for diagnosis or treatment of joint effusion, inflammatory arthritis, osteoarthritis (M16.x, M17.x),
  crystal arthropathy or joint pain (M25.5x) after failure of conservative therapy (documented).
- 20610/20611 apply to major joints (shoulder, hip, knee, subacromial bursa). 20611 includes ultrasound guidance
  and permanent recording; 77002 may be added only to 20610 when fluoroscopy is documented.
- Right/left modifiers (RT, LT) or modifier 50 for bilateral are required.
- Injected drugs are billed separately with a J-code and the NDC in the shaded supplemental area of item 24
  (qualifier N4, e.g. "N4 00186071801 UN1.000").
- Limits per joint per plan year are in each plan's Schedule of Benefits.
