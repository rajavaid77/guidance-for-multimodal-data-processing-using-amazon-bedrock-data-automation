# AP-150 Telehealth Services
Applies to: all AnyHealth plans · Synthetic demo policy

- Covered telehealth services are synchronous audio-video visits (modifier **95**) and, for established patients,
  audio-only visits (modifier **93**).
- Place of service must be **02** (telehealth other than the patient's home) or **10** (telehealth in the patient's
  home). A line with modifier 95 or 93 and an in-person POS (e.g. 11) is inconsistent and is pended for correction
  (CARC 16, RARC M77).
- The originating site, the member's consent and the modality used must be documented.
- Telehealth visits use the same cost sharing as in-person office visits.
