# AP-110 Prior Authorization Requirements
Applies to: all AnyHealth plans · Synthetic demo policy

The prior authorization number must be entered in item 23. Services rendered without a required authorization are
denied (CARC 197 precertification/authorization absent; RARC N54). Retro-authorization may be requested within
14 days of an urgent service.

| Service | AnyHealth Plus | AnyHealth Premium | AnyHealth Standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| CT, MRI, PET (70450-78816) | Required | Required | Required |
| Echocardiography (93306-93308) | Not required | Not required | Required |
| Cardiac stress testing (93015-93018) | Not required | Not required | Required |
| Chiropractic manipulation | After visit 12 | After visit 20 | After visit 6 |
| Acupuncture | Excluded | After visit 12 | Excluded |
| PT/OT | After visit 20 | After visit 30 | After visit 12 |
| Chest wall oscillation (94669) | Not required | Not required | Required |
| Infusion supplies (A4221-A4223) | Not required | Not required | Required |
| Inpatient admission, elective surgery | Required | Required | Required |
