# MP-201 Chiropractic Manipulative Treatment (CMT)
Applies to: all AnyHealth plans · Synthetic demo policy · Codes: 98940, 98941, 98942, 98943

## Coverage criteria
CMT is medically necessary when ALL apply:
1. A neuromusculoskeletal condition of the spine (e.g. M54.2 cervicalgia, M54.5x low back pain, M54.9 dorsalgia,
   M99.0x segmental dysfunction) documented with onset date and functional deficits.
2. A written treatment plan with measurable goals, frequency and expected duration.
3. Objective improvement is documented at least every 12 visits (re-evaluation).

## Code selection
- 98940: 1-2 spinal regions; 98941: 3-4 regions; 98942: 5 regions. The number of regions treated must be documented.
- 98943 (extraspinal) is covered only with a documented extraspinal diagnosis.
- Acute or active corrective care must carry modifier **AT**. Maintenance or wellness care (no expectation of
  improvement) is **not medically necessary** and is denied (CARC 50).

## Related services
- An E/M visit on the same day as CMT requires modifier **25** and a separately identifiable problem.
- Spinal X-rays are covered when required to rule out contraindications (trauma, red-flag symptoms, age > 50 with
  new onset), and must use a region consistent with the spinal diagnosis.
- Massage (97124) on the same date is covered only per MP-203.

## Documentation
A treatment note signed by the chiropractor for every date of service, listing regions treated, subjective
complaints, objective findings and response to treatment.
