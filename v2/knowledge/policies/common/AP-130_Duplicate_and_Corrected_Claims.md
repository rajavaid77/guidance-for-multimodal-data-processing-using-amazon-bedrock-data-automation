# AP-130 Duplicate and Corrected Claims
Applies to: all AnyHealth plans · Synthetic demo policy

- An exact duplicate is the same member, rendering/billing provider, date of service, procedure code, modifier and
  units as a line already processed. Exact duplicates are denied (CARC 18 exact duplicate claim/service).
- A repeat service on the same date is payable only with an appropriate modifier: 76 (repeat procedure same
  physician), 77 (repeat by another physician) or 91 (repeat laboratory test), and supporting documentation.
- Corrected claims must use resubmission code 7 or 8 in item 22 (AP-100).
