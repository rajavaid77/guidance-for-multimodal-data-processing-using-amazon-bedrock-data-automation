# AnyHealth Premium - Schedule of Benefits (Plan Year 2025)

Document ID: SOB-PREMIUM-2025 · Applies to: AnyHealth Premium members · Synthetic demo document.

## 1. Precedence
This Schedule of Benefits clarifies and, where they conflict, supersedes the AnyHealth Premium Evidence of Coverage.
