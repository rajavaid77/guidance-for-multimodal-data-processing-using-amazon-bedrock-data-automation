# AnyHealth Standard - Schedule of Benefits (Plan Year 2025)

Document ID: SOB-STANDARD-2025 · Applies to: AnyHealth Standard members · Synthetic demo document.

## 1. Precedence
This Schedule of Benefits clarifies and, where they conflict, supersedes the AnyHealth Standard Evidence of Coverage.
The EoC lists acupuncture and chiropractic services under "services and items that are not covered" while the same
paragraphs describe them as covered. The authoritative position is:
- **Acupuncture is excluded under AnyHealth Standard.**
- **Chiropractic manipulation is covered** when medically necessary, subject to the limit and prior authorization below.

## 4. Exclusions
Acupuncture; home visits by physicians or chiropractors; massage therapy outside an active PT/chiropractic plan of
care; travel expenses unless pre-approved.
