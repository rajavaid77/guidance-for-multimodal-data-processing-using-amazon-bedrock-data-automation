# AnyHealth Plus - Schedule of Benefits (Plan Year 2025)

Document ID: SOB-PLUS-2025 · Applies to: AnyHealth Plus members · Synthetic demo document.

## 1. Precedence
This Schedule of Benefits clarifies and, where they conflict, supersedes the narrative descriptions in the
AnyHealth Plus Evidence of Coverage (EoC). Section 4 of the EoC lists acupuncture under "services and items that are
not covered"; the sentence in that paragraph describing acupuncture as covered is a drafting error. **Acupuncture is
excluded under AnyHealth Plus.**

## 4. Exclusions (in addition to EoC section 4)
